## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Key features:
1. Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Connection: Farmers can register, upload product details, add images, set prices, and sell products directly.
2. AI-Based Demand Forecasting: AI analyses previous sales, seasonal patterns, and market demand to predict which products will be required in the future.
3. Smart Price Suggestions: The platform provides recommended selling prices based on market trends and product availability.
4. Bulk Purchasing: Hotels, supermarkets, restaurants, and retailers can purchase agricultural products in large quantities directly from farmers.
5. Smart Logistics: The system connects farmers with nearby delivery partners and identifies efficient transportation routes.
6. Live Order Tracking: Farmers and customers can monitor the status of their orders.
7. Multilingual Support: The application supports regional languages to make it accessible to farmers from different backgrounds.
8. Digital Payment System: Secure online payments and digital transaction records improve transparency.

2. How it addresses the problem
- Eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by connecting farmers directly with buyers.
- Helps farmers receive a better share of the final selling price.
- Reduces consumer expenses by avoiding multiple commission charges.
- Uses AI to reduce uncertainty in crop demand and supply.
- Optimises transportation routes to reduce delivery time and fuel expenses.
- Provides transparent pricing and real-time product availability.
- Reduces agricultural product wastage through better demand and logistics planning.
  
3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution
- AI-powered agricultural marketplace: Combines direct selling with intelligent demand prediction.
- Smart crop demand alerts: Notifies farmers about products that are expected to have high demand.
- Fair price recommendation: Helps farmers avoid selling products at unnecessarily low prices.
- Nearby buyer matching: Automatically identifies potential buyers based on location and product availability.
- Shared transportation: Combines orders from nearby locations to reduce transportation costs.
- Farmer-friendly interface: Simple design with regional language support and voice-based assistance.
- Waste reduction: Connects farmers with nearby buyers when products are at risk of remaining unsold.
Unlike a normal shopping application, FARMCONNECT focuses on improving the entire agricultural supply chain, from the farmer's field to the consumer's home.

1. Farmer registers on the platform.
2. Farmer uploads crop details, quantity, location, and expected price.
3. AI analyses market trends and predicts demand.
4. Buyers search for products and place orders.
5. The platform matches buyers with suitable farmers.
6. The logistics module finds an efficient delivery route.
7. Farmers confirm orders and dispatch products.
8. Consumers receive products and complete payment.
9. The system collects transaction data to improve future predictions.

## Feasibility and Viability
Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
FARMCONNECT is technically and economically feasible because it uses widely available technologies such as smartphones, internet connectivity, cloud computing, and open-source machine learning tools.
- Farmers can access the platform using smartphones.
- Buyers can place orders through a website or mobile application.
- AI models can be trained using historical market and sales data.
- Existing transport services can be integrated for product delivery.
- The platform can initially be launched in a small agricultural region and gradually expanded.
Potential challenges and risks
1. Lack of digital knowledge among some farmers.
2. Poor internet connectivity in rural areas.
3. Difficulty in maintaining product quality during transportation.
4. Fluctuations in agricultural market prices.
5. Trust issues between farmers and buyers.
6. High transportation costs for small orders.
Strategies for overcoming these challenges
- Provide a simple interface with regional language and voice assistance.
- Support low-bandwidth access and SMS notifications.
- Introduce product quality verification and buyer feedback.
- Update price recommendations using current market information.
- Use verified farmer and buyer accounts.
- Combine nearby orders to reduce transportation costs.
- Provide customer support and transparent payment records.
